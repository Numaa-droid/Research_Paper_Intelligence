How a run works

Watch the demo →
  1. Ingest and index. The PDF becomes page-aware chunks behind a hybrid index.
  2. Plan. The planner picks specialists based on what the paper contains.
  3. Analyse in parallel. Summary, methodology, experiments and math agents cite quotes.
  4. Verify and retry. Claims are re-checked; weak agents re-run with feedback.
  5. Report. Grounded findings are written up; unsupported ones are listed openly.

What happens next

  1. Read and indexText, sections, tables and equations are extracted; scanned pages go through OCR.
  2. PlanThe planner decides which specialists this paper needs.
  3. Analyse in parallelEach agent writes findings with the exact quote behind them.
  4. VerifyQuotes must exist in the PDF and numbers must match, or the claim is flagged.
  5. ReportA Markdown report with page citations, plus a list of what couldn't be grounded.
Tip: text-based PDFs give the most precise citations. Your papers and reports are visible only to your account.
